How to reach IIT Bombay

Powai, Mumbai 400076

Campus map

Head for the Main Gate

Every route below ends at the Main Gate (Gate 2). Ask the driver or bus conductor for "IIT Main Gate". See it on the map

Address: IIT Bombay, Powai, Mumbai 400076, Maharashtra, India
Coming for Techfest or Mood Indigo?Entry rules, crowds and getting home
  • Register before you come, and carry your pass or entry band and a photo ID. When the campus fills up, security lets in only registered participants and residents, and walk-ins are turned away.
  • Check the fest's website or Instagram on the day for entry rules and gate changes: techfest.org, moodi.org.
  • Before the big evening shows, thousands gather at the Main Gate and traffic stops on JVLR and around Powai. Come early in the day, by train to Kanjurmarg. If your auto gets stuck, get down and walk the rest.
  • Halls close their doors when full, and popular talks and shows have long queues. Join the queue early.
  • Phone networks slow down in big crowds. Open the campus map once before you come, and it works offline.
  • Shows end late. Check the last train in m-Indicator, and expect long waits and surge prices for cabs. Autos charge 25% extra after midnight.

1. Where are you coming from?

Tap your starting point for the steps. Fares are approximate; see the rate card.

Buy train tickets at the station counter or in the RailOne or Mumbai One app, metro tickets at the station or in Mumbai One, and bus tickets from the conductor or in the Chalo app. Get the apps.

Airport

Terminal 1 (domestic)11 km. Auto ~₹200, or bus 422

Easiest: auto rickshaw to IIT Main Gate.

By bus:

  1. Walk to the Domestic Airport Junction bus stop.
  2. Take bus 422 straight to IIT Main Gate.

Or bus 182 to Gandhi Nagar (Kanjurmarg), then bus 524 to IIT Main Gate.

By metro: the Aqua Line from CSMIA T1 station to Aarey JVLR, then an auto.

Terminal 2 (international)8 km. Auto ~₹150, or prepaid taxi

Easiest: auto rickshaw or prepaid taxi to IIT Main Gate.

By bus: 409 Ltd or 382 AS to IIT Main Gate.

By metro: the Aqua Line from CSMIA T2 station to Aarey JVLR, then an auto.

Navi Mumbai (NMIA)Cab, 1-2 hours. Or bus and trains

Easiest: a cab (Uber, Ola or the airport taxi) to IIT Main Gate, 1-2 hours depending on traffic.

By bus and train:

  1. Take the NMMT AC airport bus to Belapur CBD station.
  2. Buy a ticket to Kanjurmarg.
  3. Take a Harbour line train towards CSMT, and get down at Kurla.
  4. Change to a slow Central line train towards Thane.
  5. Get down at Kanjurmarg and follow the Kanjurmarg steps.

NMMT airport buses also run to Nerul, Targhar, Kharkopar and Khandeshwar stations.

Metro

Aqua Line (Metro Line 3)From South Mumbai, Dadar, BKC or the airport. Metro to Aarey JVLR, then auto ~₹100
  1. Take the Aqua Line towards Aarey JVLR, the last stop. It runs through Cuffe Parade, Churchgate, CSMT, Mumbai Central, Dadar, BKC and both airport terminals.
  2. Get down at Aarey JVLR.
  3. Take an auto to IIT Main Gate, ~₹100.

Train and metro fares rise with distance. The whole Aqua Line, Cuffe Parade to Aarey JVLR, is ₹70.

Auto and taxi fares are the MMRTA meter rates from 1 September 2026. If a meter still shows the old fare, the driver must work out the new one from the RTO tariff card. Uber, Ola and Rapido set their own prices. RailOne and Mumbai One show exact train and metro fares.

For visitors from abroad: ₹100 is about US$1, so a 10 km auto ride is about US$2.

Good to know

  • Autos don't run south of Bandra and Sion. At Mumbai Central, CST or Dadar, take a train, the metro or a black-and-yellow taxi.
  • Ask auto drivers to go by the meter. If one refuses, book an auto in Uber, Ola or Rapido.
  • Only slow trains stop at Kanjurmarg. The platform display shows S for slow and F for fast.
  • Trains are packed towards CSMT and Churchgate 8-11 AM, and the other way 5-8 PM. With luggage, avoid those hours or take a cab.
  • Some coaches are for women only, and are marked on the train and the platform. A second class ticket is not valid in first class.
  • Keep away from the open door, and keep your phone inside the coach.
  • In the monsoon (June to September), heavy rain can flood roads and slow or stop trains. Check m-Indicator and leave extra time.

2. At the gate

Security checks visitors at the gate. Keep a photo ID and your invitation or registration email ready.

3. Get to your building

The Main Building is about 1 km from the Main Gate, and the hostels 1.5-2.6 km. Shared autos run inside the campus for ₹10 a seat, and there are buggies (golf carts) too.

The campus map has walking directions from the gate to any building, hostel or room. It works offline once opened.
